 Key Insights

 What is the AI Stack?
1. Layer 1 - Compute & Foundation Models: The engine (rented APIs like OpenAI, Anthropic)
2. Layer 2 - Data: The moat (proprietary data that makes AI uniquely valuable)
3. Layer 3 - Deployment:The product (how users interact with AI)
4. Layer 4 - Observability: Risk management (monitoring quality & security)



How This Applies to IlimiAI

Our Layer 1 (The Engine):
- Will use AI APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source models)
- Cost consideration: Track cost per student query

Our Layer 2 (THE MOAT):
- 1,000+ hours of Hausa explanation data
- JAMB/WAEC past questions (2010-2026)
- Exam pattern data (trap options, 200 high-yield topics)
- Individual student learning speeds & preferences

Our Layer 3 (The Product):
- Voice + Text interface
- Adaptive learning paths
- English ↔ Hausa ↔ Pidgin switching

Our Layer 4 (Risk Management):
- Monitor: Wrong answers on past questions
- Monitor: Voice API failures
- Monitor: Student frustration signals



CEO Takeaway
"My competitive advantage isn't the AI model itself (anyone can rent GPT-4). 

My moat is the proprietary JAMB/WAEC data and 1,000+ hours of Hausa teaching data. 

The model is the engine; the data is the fuel."
